# Retail Sales Business Performance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data to evaluate business performance, identify revenue drivers, and generate actionable business insights using Python.

The analysis focuses on answering real business questions rather than only technical exploration.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions
1. Which products generate the highest revenue?
2. What sales trends exist over time?
3. Which customer segments contribute most to sales?
4. What categories drive business growth?
5. Where should the company focus strategically?

---

## 🛠 Tools & Skills
- Python
- Pandas
- Data Cleaning
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Data Visualization
- Business Insight Generation

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- Revenue is concentrated among a small number of product categories.
- Seasonal trends significantly impact sales performance.
- High-value customers contribute disproportionately to revenue.
- Certain categories underperform despite high volume.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations
- Focus marketing on top-performing categories.
- Improve strategy for low-margin products.
- Target high-value customer segments.
- Optimize inventory based on sales trends.
